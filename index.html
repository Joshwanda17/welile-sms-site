Welile SMS Forwarder

Mobile money intake for Android.

Download APK

Android 8.0 or newer. Internal Welile use.

Install

  1. Open the downloaded file. If Android asks, allow installs from your browser ("Install unknown apps").
  2. Open SMS Forwarder and grant the SMS permissions when prompted.
  3. Disable battery optimisation for the app so it keeps running in the background.

Setup

  1. In the Setup card, confirm the endpoint URL is https://byrzshwlentqvktcksem.supabase.co/functions/v1/sms-forwarder-ingest.
  2. Enter the device token you were given. Ask your Welile admin if you don't have one.
  3. Tap Save and enable. The status pill turns Active.

Only MTNMobMoney and AirtelMoney messages are forwarded. OTP and secret-code messages are never sent.
